description=[h1]LX Split-Screen Quick Build[/h1]
description=B41-style build menu for Project Zomboid Build 42 (B42), made for splitscreen / split screen / local couch co-op where the B42 build window does not fit. Works with mouse and with a controller or gamepad.
description=B42's build window is wider than half a screen. In split-screen you simply cannot use it. This puts building back in the context menu.
description=
description=[h2]What it does[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Build from the context menu[/b] - Right click the world, or press the context-menu button on a controller, and pick [b]Quick build[/b]. No window, no scrolling, no viewport problem.
description=[*][b]Everything that matters, grouped[/b] - Walls, frames, doors, floors, fences and stairs in wood (Carpentry), metal (Metalworking) and brick/stone (Masonry), plus Furniture and Outdoors.
description=[*][b]Tooltips that answer the question[/b] - Each entry shows the skill it needs and your level, whether the recipe still has to be learned from a book, every material and tool with how much you are…
description=[*][b]Same engine as the vanilla window[/b] - It drives the game's own BuildLogic and placement cursor, so skill checks, recipe validity, material consumption and the build action itself are identical to…
description=[*][b]Keeps building[/b] - After you place a piece the cursor stays armed for the next one, like B41. B cancels. D-pad moves, LB/RB rotate, A builds.
description=[/list]
description=[b]Requires:[/b] Nothing. Vanilla B42.21 is enough.
description=[b]Install:[/b] Subscribe, then enable it under Mods in the main menu. In split-screen, enabling it once covers every player.
description=[b]Options:[/b] Options → MODS → [b]LX Split-Screen Quick Build[/b]. Show or hide the menu, and show or hide the available/total counters.
